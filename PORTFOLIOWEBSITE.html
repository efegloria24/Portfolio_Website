Sales Performance Analysis

Business Question

Where are revenue leaks occurring across regions, and which product lines are dragging down overall profit margins?

Data & Tools

Multi-table relational transactions database analyzed using Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and Excel.

Key Findings

  • Total revenue analyzed reached ₦2.33B across 2,098 unique customers.
  • 15% of product catalog items generated 80% of total company profit.
  • Specific high-volume regional hubs suffered negative profit margins due to shipping inefficiencies.

Recommendations

Restructure logistics routes in underperforming territories and reallocate marketing focus toward top 15% performing product SKUs to expand gross margins.

TechNova Gadgets Analysis

Business Question

Are high discount rates driving profitable customer retention, or simply eroding product margins?

Data & Tools

Customer transaction histories and e-commerce event logs evaluated using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

Key Findings

  • Promotions increased initial order volume but failed to improve second-time repeat purchase rates.
  • Discounts were heavily consumed on low-margin accessories rather than high-margin flagship hardware.

Recommendations

Transition from blanket site-wide discounts to targeted post-purchase retention sequences bundling accessories directly with flagship purchases.

Logistics Operations Database Capstone

Business Question

How can operational bottlenecks and shipment latency be monitored and reduced in real time?

Data & Tools

Normalized Relational SQL Schema, PostgreSQL CTEs, Window Functions, Power BI Dashboard.

Key Findings

  • Delivery delay spikes occurred consistently during specific peak dispatch hours due to batch allocation constraints.
  • On-Time In-Full (OTIF) delivery metrics dropped 18% during peak hours.

Recommendations

Implement staggered dispatch schedules and set automated SQL threshold alerts for delayed route dispatches.
